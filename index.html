How we work

Assess. Deploy. Manage. Protect.

One method for every engagement, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Assess

    We look at what you have, what it costs and where the risks are, then hand you a ranked, written plan.

  2. Deploy

    We build it right the first time, on a written scope and price, with every change documented.

  3. Manage

    We keep it running: users, devices, updates, vendors and a real person when something breaks.

  4. Protect

    We keep watch, test the backups, train your people and keep the evidence ready for audits.
